Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Pump Check is a student team project built for a software engineering course. It is a demonstration, not a commercial service. Please use test information and do not enter anything you would not want others on the demo to see.

1. Who we are

Pump Check is run by Team J.U.R.K.K.S, a group of students. You can reach us at mkjurks@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a username you choose, and your password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth). We never see it or store it in our own tables.
  • Content you add: gym reviews (ratings, text, busy-time notes, and optional photos), equipment reports, workout logs, workout plans, calorie logs, and reports you file about reviews.
  • Your location, only if you choose to share it: if you press "Use my location," your browser sends your coordinates to our server so it can sort gyms by distance for that search. We do not save them to your profile.
  • Technical information: your login token is kept in your browser's session storage until you log out. Our server records error logs when something fails.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your information.

3. What other people can see

  • Visible to other signed-in users: your username with your reviews, review text and ratings, equipment reports, and any workout plans you mark public.
  • Review photos are public. Photos are stored in a public bucket, so anyone who has a photo's web address can open it, even without an account. Do not upload photos that show identifiable people without their permission, and do not upload anything private.
  • Private to you: your email, workout logs, calorie logs, and private plans.
  • Team members who maintain the database may be able to see stored data while keeping the demo running.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your history, and calculate gym ratings.
  • To check workout plans against a gym's reported equipment.
  • To review reported content and keep the community usable.
  • To find and fix bugs.

5. Who we share it with

  • Supabase provides our sign-in, database, and photo storage.
  • Our hosting provider (for example, Render) runs our server.
  • USDA FoodData Central receives the text of your food searches from our server. We do not send your name, email, or account details with them.

We do not share your information for advertising and do not sell it. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Health-related information

Workout logs and calorie logs describe your fitness and eating habits. Entering them is optional, they are not shown to other users, and they are not medical advice. See our Terms of Use.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account exists. Because this is a course project, we may erase all demo data after the course ends. Pump Check does not yet have a delete-account button. To ask us to delete your account, your reviews, logs, plans, and photos, email mkjurks@gmail.com from the address on your account, and we will do it within a reasonable time.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are protected by Supabase Auth, and database rules limit who can change what. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive information in reviews or plans.

9. Your choices

  • Change your username or password on the Account page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing mkjurks@gmail.com.
  • California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law. We are a student project, we do not sell or share personal information, and we will consider any reasonable request.

10. Children

Pump Check is for people aged 13 and older. If you are younger, please do not create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed up, we will delete the account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
